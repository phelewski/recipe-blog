## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes (plus 10 minutes standing)
- **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 large English cucumber (about 1 lb.), thinly sliced (⅛ inch thick)
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic (1 large clove, minced), optional
- 1 tablespoon gochugaru, or to taste (or ½ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes)
- ¼ cup rice wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds, optional

## Instructions

1. Place the cucumber slices in a bowl
2. In a small bowl, combine the remaining ingredients and stir until sugar and salt have dissolved
3. Pour over the cucumbers and toss to coat
4. Let stand 10 minutes before serving. Refrigerate until ready to serve
