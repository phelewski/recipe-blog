## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** TBD (depends on topping and oven)
- **Total Time:** 1 hour 15 minutes (plus rising time)
- **Servings:** 1 (14-inch pan pizza)

## Ingredients

### Sauce

Pantry

- 15oz tomato sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried basil
- ½ tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

### Dough

Pantry

- 375g bread flour
- 208g water (105°F)
- 4.5g active dry yeast
- 6.5g salt
- 16g safflower oil
- 7g sugar
- 9g dairy powder

## Instructions

### Make the dough

1. Mix all ingredients until combined and let rest for 10 minutes
2. Knead for 5 minutes at speed 4
3. Let rest in bowl for 1 hour

### Make the sauce

1. Combine all sauce ingredients in a small pot and simmer on low to reduce slightly for 30 minutes

### Bake

1. After resting, shape the dough into a 14-inch pan pizza
2. Spread the Pizza Hut sauce over the dough
3. Add your desired toppings
4. Bake according to your oven and topping preferences
