## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 12 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes (chilling)
- **Total Time:** 42 minutes
- **Servings:** 8

## Ingredients

Pantry

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 6 Tbsp. sugar
- ¼ cup cider vinegar
- 1½ tsp. kosher salt
- 1 tsp. black pepper

Produce

- 1 small green cabbage head (about 2 lbs.)
- 2 large carrots (about 5 oz.)
- 1 small Vidalia onion (about 6 oz.)

## Instructions

### Make the dressing

1. Whisk together mayonnaise, sugar, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a large bowl
2. Refrigerate until ready to use

### Prepare the vegetables

1. Cut cabbage head into quarters
2. Shred cabbage on the large holes of a box grater
3. Shred carrots and onion on the box grater

### Mix and chill

1. Add cabbage, carrots, and onion to the mayonnaise mixture
2. Stir well to combine
3. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving
