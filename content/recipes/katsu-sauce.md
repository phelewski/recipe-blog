## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 4 tablespoons

## Ingredients

Pantry

- ¼ cup ketchup
- 1 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
- ½ Tbsp. regular soy sauce
- ½ Tbsp. mirin
- ½ Tbsp. white granulated sugar
- 1 tsp. dark brown sugar
- ½ tsp. garlic powder
- ¼ tsp. ginger powder

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, add all the ingredients: ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, mirin, white granulated sugar, dark brown sugar, garlic powder, and ginger powder
2. Whisk until fully incorporated
3. Serve with chicken or pork katsu
