## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** TBD (depends on oven)
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes (plus rising time)
- **Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

### Breadstick Dough

Pantry

- 500g bread flour
- 4.5g salt
- 24g dairy powder
- 15.5g sugar
- 4.5g active dry yeast
- 320g water
- 30g safflower oil
- 4 Tbsp. butter (for coating pan)

### Breadstick Topping

Pantry

- 2 Tbsp. grated Parmesan
- 1 Tbsp. onion powder
- 1 Tbsp. dried oregano
- 1¾ tsp. garlic powder
- 1½ tsp. dried basil
- ½ tsp. kosher salt
- Melted butter (for coating after baking)

## Instructions

### Make the dough

1. Mix all dough ingredients in the bowl on speed 4 for 5 minutes
2. Coat pan with 4 Tbsp. butter
3. Once dough is stretched into the pan, top with melted butter to cover surface

### Bake and finish

1. Bake until golden brown
2. After breadsticks come out of the oven, coat with melted butter and sprinkle with topping mixture (Parmesan, onion powder, oregano, garlic powder, basil, kosher salt)
