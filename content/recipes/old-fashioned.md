## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 drink

## Ingredients

- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
- ¼ oz rich (2:1) simple syrup or demerara syrup (or 1-2 teaspoons sugar + 1 teaspoon water)
- 2 to 3 dashes Angostura aromatic bitters
- Ice (large cubes slow dilution)
- 1 strip orange peel (and optional cocktail cherry)

## Instructions

1. **Combine base:** Add whiskey, syrup (or sugar and water), and bitters to a mixing glass with ice.
2. **Chill:** Stir for 20 to 30 seconds until well-chilled and slightly diluted.
3. **Strain:** Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube or fresh ice.
4. **Garnish:** Twist the orange peel over the drink to express its oils, drop it in, and add a cherry if desired.
