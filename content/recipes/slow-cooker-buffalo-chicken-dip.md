## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 2 hours
- **Total Time:** 2 hours 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 8

## Ingredients

Dairy

- 8 oz. full-fat cream cheese
- 1½ cups freshly shredded cheddar cheese, divided (about 150g)
- Optional: freshly crumbled blue cheese for serving

Meat

- 3 cups shredded cooked chicken, packed (about 400g; rotisserie or canned works)

Pantry

- ¾ cup buffalo wing sauce like Frank's (about 180ml)
- ½ cup ranch dressing (about 120ml)
- ½ cup blue cheese dressing or more ranch (about 120ml)
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. onion powder
- ¼ tsp. white pepper or black pepper, more to taste

Garnish

- 2 Tbsp. freshly snipped chives or green onions (about 8g)
- Chips, pretzels, veggies, etc. for dipping

## Instructions

### Slow Cooker Method

1. To the bowl of a slow cooker, add the cream cheese, shredded chicken, buffalo wing sauce, ranch dressing, blue cheese dressing, 1 cup of shredded cheddar cheese, garlic powder, onion powder, and white or black pepper
2. Stir to combine
3. Cover the slow cooker and set it to LOW for 1½–2 hours, or until the cream cheese is completely melted and the dip is hot
4. Stir the dip often to prevent burning. Taste and add more spices, if needed
5. When the dip is done cooking, sprinkle the top with the remaining ½ cup of shredded cheddar cheese
6. Cover and cook for 15–20 more minutes, or until the cheese is completely melted

### Broil in Oven (Optional)

For a crispy top: when the dip is done, grease a small baking dish, spoon the dip into the baking dish, and sprinkle the top with the remaining ½ cup of shredded cheddar cheese. Broil on HIGH until the cheese is bubbly

### Serve

1. Garnish the finished dip with the chives or green onions and the blue cheese crumbles, if using
2. Serve with chips, pretzels, veggies, and other dippers

## Notes

- I like Frank's RedHot Wing sauce in this recipe. If you like extra spicy dip, use extra hot wing sauce. For a milder dip, make sure to buy the mild sauce
- For the best flavor, I like good-quality ranch and blue cheese dressing from the refrigerator aisle. It adds so much extra flavor!
- If you're not a fan of blue cheese, feel free to leave it out. Just replace the blue cheese dressing with more ranch and omit the blue cheese crumbles entirely
- The cook time will depend on your slow cooker. Mine cooks super fast, so I set this dip to LOW for 1½–2 hours. If your crock-pot cooks slow, cook the dip on HIGH for 1½–2 hours
