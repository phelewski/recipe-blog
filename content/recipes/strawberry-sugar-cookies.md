## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes (plus 2 hours chilling)
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes (plus chilling time)
- **Servings:** 30 cookies

## Ingredients

### Strawberry Sugar Cookies

Dairy

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter (softened)

Pantry

- 2 large eggs (room temperature)
- 3 cups (375g) all-purpose flour
- 2 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 2 tsp. cream of tartar
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1½ cups (300g) granulated sugar
- 1 tsp. pure vanilla extract

Produce

- 6 Tbsp. (36g) freeze-dried strawberry powder
- 1 tsp. pure strawberry extract (Amoretti Artisan Natural Wild Strawberry or Cooks)
- ½ tsp. red gel food coloring

### Sparkling Strawberry Sugar

- ½ cup white or red sparkling sugar (sanding sugar)
- 1 Tbsp. freeze-dried strawberry powder

## Instructions

### Prepare the freeze-dried strawberry powder

1. Blend or process the strawberries in a high-speed blender or spice grinder until they become a fine powder
2. Mix together with the sugar for the sparkling strawberry sugar (2 tablespoons of fruit powder for every ½ cup of sparkling sugar)
3. Store in an airtight container in a cool dry location until ready to use

### Make the cookie dough

1. Remove butter and eggs from refrigerator
2. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper; set aside
3. In the bowl of a stand mixer, cream the butter and granulated sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes
4. Add the eggs, one at a time, and blend well with each addition
5. Add vanilla extract, strawberry extract and red gel food coloring and mix until well blended
6. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, cream of tartar, salt and strawberry fruit powder until well blended
7. Add the flour mixture and blend on low speed just until mixed — do not overmix (this will keep your cookies soft and tender)

### Chill and shape

1. Chill cookie dough for at least 2 hours and up to overnight
2. Place Sparkling Strawberry Sugar in a small bowl
3. Using a medium cookie scoop, scoop into 30 cookie dough balls
4. Roll each piece of dough into a ball in the palms of your hands, and then roll in Sparkling Strawberry Sugar until evenly coated
5. Place each sugared cookie ball onto prepared cookie sheets, at least 2 inches apart

### Bake

1. Bake the cookies in pre-heated oven for about 10 minutes, or until the tops are just beginning to set
2. Remove pans from oven and allow to cool for 5 minutes on baking sheet before transferring to a cooling rack to cool completely
3. For bakery-style look: sprinkle with additional Sparkling Sugar as soon as you remove from the oven, which will settle into all the crackly cookie tops as the cookies cool

## Notes

### Tips for Best Results

- **Use soft butter!** For best texture, make sure to use good quality unsalted butter that is softened
- **Don't overmix!** The secret to buttery and tender sugar cookies is to be careful not to overmix the cookie dough when adding the flour
- **Gel food coloring** — I prefer to use gel food coloring or powdered food coloring when baking, as it's a high concentration and you can use less than you would of traditional liquid food coloring

### Storage

- These cookies will stay soft and fresh in an air-tight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerated for up to 1 week
- **To freeze:** These cookies can be frozen for up to 3 months. After baking and cooling, place them in an airtight, freezer safe container. If you need to layer the cookies, place parchment paper in between each layer to avoid sticking or freezing together

### Parties

I package cookies individually in self-sealing clear cellophane bags (Pack It Chic are my favorite brand). Make sure cookies are completely cool before packaging

### Recipe Variations

This same concept and recipe can be used for your favorite fruit sugar cookies and sparkling fruit sugar! Substitute your favorite freeze-dried fruit and/or fruit flavor:

- Raspberry Sugar Cookies
- Blueberry Sugar Cookies
- Mango Sugar Cookies
- Banana Sugar Cookies
- Apple Sugar Cookies (try sprinkling with cinnamon sugar!)
- Cranberry Sugar Cookies
- Sweet Peach Sugar Cookies
- Watermelon Sugar Cookies
