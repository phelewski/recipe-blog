## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes (plus 8 hours chilling)
- **Servings:** 40 (makes about 5 cups)

## Ingredients

Dairy

- 32 oz. plain low-fat yogurt

Produce

- 1 English cucumber (with peel, grated)
- 1 clove garlic (pressed)
- Fresh dill for garnish

Pantry

- 2 Tbsp. fresh lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 Tbsp. chopped fresh dill
- 1 Tbsp. salt, or to taste
- 1 Tbsp. freshly ground black pepper, or to taste
- 2 tsp. grated lemon zest

## Instructions

1. Combine yogurt, grated cucumber, lemon juice, olive oil, and garlic in a large bowl
2. Add dill, salt, pepper, and lemon zest; mix until smooth
3. Pour into a serving dish and garnish with fresh dill

## Notes

For the best flavor, cover tightly and refrigerate for 8 hours before serving to let the flavors blend
