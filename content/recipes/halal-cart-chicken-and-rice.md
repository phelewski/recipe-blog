## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus marinating time)
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Total Time:** 1 hour
- **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

**Chicken and Marinade:**

- 600 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 10 g kosher salt
- 5 g ground cumin
- 3 g ground coriander
- 3 g ground black pepper
- 3 g dried oregano
- 3 g onion powder
- 3 g garlic powder
- 25 g plain yogurt (not Greek)
- 15 g fresh lemon juice
- 15 g olive oil, plus extra for searing

**Rice:**

- 200 g basmati rice
- 3 Tbsp oil
- 15 g unsalted butter
- 2 g ground turmeric
- 400 g chicken stock
- 25 g Better Than Bouillon, chicken
- 5 g onion powder
- 5 g garlic powder

**White Sauce:**

- 125 g mayonnaise
- 125 g plain yogurt (not Greek)
- 10 g lemon juice
- 3 g kosher salt
- 5 g garlic, minced

**Red Sauce:**

- 120 g Sriracha
- 60 g hot sauce (Louisiana brand)

**To Serve:**

- Head lettuce (Little Gem, romaine, or iceberg), medium diced
- Tomatoes, small diced

## Instructions

1. **Make the marinade:** Whisk yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and all the spices into a smooth paste.
2. **Marinate the chicken:** Toss chicken thighs in the marinade until fully coated. Refrigerate at least 30–60 minutes if you have time. Otherwise, just marinate while the rice cooks.
3. **Preheat the oven** to 425°F / 220°C.
4. **Rinse the basmati rice** in a colander until the water runs clear. Drain well.
5. Heat 3–4 Tbsp oil in a 3–4 quart saucepan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the rice and toast 4 to 5 minutes, stirring constantly, until nearly all the grains are opaque and a few are starting to toast.
6. Stir in butter and turmeric until the butter is fully melted and the rice turns yellow. Add chicken stock, Better Than Bouillon, garlic powder, and onion powder. Stir until the paste dissolves.
7. Bring to a gentle boil, give it one final stir, cover and reduce the heat to low (about 2 or 3 out of 10). Cook 15 minutes.
8. Remove from the heat and steam, covered, 15 minutes more. Keep the lid on the whole time, then fluff with a fork and set aside until time to serve. Do not stir the rice or lift the lid during its 30-minute cook time.
9. **While the rice cooks, sear the chicken:** Heat a 12-inch oven-safe stainless steel sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add a generous amount of oil, then add thighs smooth side down. Press down with a weight or spatula, then sear undisturbed for 4 to 5 minutes or until deeply golden brown.
10. Once a nice golden sear has developed, flip the thighs and sear for 1–2 minutes until color begins to develop, then transfer the pan to the oven and roast 7 to 9 minutes. They're done when the thickest thigh reads 175 to 185°F (85°C) or even a little higher.
11. **While the chicken roasts, make the white sauce:** Whisk mayonnaise, yogurt, salt, minced garlic, and lemon juice until smooth and pourable. Taste and adjust with a little more lemon or salt if needed.
12. Prep the rest of your toppings by mixing together the red sauce ingredients and dicing lettuce and tomatoes.
13. **Rest the chicken:** When the thighs are done, rest them on a plate for about 10 minutes. Meanwhile, add roughly 2 tablespoons of water to the pan and scrape up the brown bits to form a chicken jus. If there are any burnt or blackened bits of marinade in the pan, pull those out with a fork before making your jus.
14. Pass the rested thighs through the pan jus, then dice into ¾-inch chunks. Drip some of the rested chicken drippings over the diced chicken if you want.
15. **To assemble:** Spoon rice into bowls and top with chicken, diced lettuce and tomatoes, and a drizzle of white sauce and red sauce.

## Notes/Tips

- **Make it ahead:** The marinade can be applied up to 24 hours in advance for deeper flavor.
- **The jus is key:** Don't skip scraping the brown bits from the pan — that's where all the flavor lives. It makes the chicken juicy and adds a rich sauce over the rice.
- **Customize your heat:** Adjust the red sauce ratio to taste — more Sriracha for tangy heat, or more Louisiana-style hot sauce for vinegar-forward spice.
- This recipe is inspired by the famous chicken and rice platters from NYC halal carts. Adapted from Brian Lagerstrom.
