## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 drink

## Ingredients

- 2 oz. tequila
- 2 oz. grapefruit juice (fresh, plus wedges for garnish)
- 2 oz. sparkling water
- ½ oz. lime juice (fresh)
  > Substitute 4-6 oz. of grapefruit soda instead of the grapefruit juice, sparkling water, and lime juice
- ¼ oz. agave nectar or simple syrup, or to taste
- Coarse sea salt, for the rim of the glasses
- Ice

## Instructions

1. Salt the rim of the glass by rubbing a grapefruit wedge around the edge and dipping it onto a small plate of salt
2. Mix the tequila, grapefruit juice, sparkling water, lime juice, and agave nectar into the glass
3. Fill the remainder of the glass with ice
4. Adjust sweetness to taste
5. Garnish with grapefruit wedge
