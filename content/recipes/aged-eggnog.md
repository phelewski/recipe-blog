## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 90 days (aging) + 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 8–10

## Ingredients

### Phase 1 (Base Mix)

Dairy

- 1 cup whole milk

Liquor

- 1 cup Jamaican rum (Appleton Estates 43% ABV)
- 1 cup cognac (Courvoisier VS 40% ABV)
- 1 cup bourbon (Old Forrester 50% ABV)

Other

- 12 large eggs (yolks only)
- 1 pound sugar
- ⅔ tsp. nutmeg (grated, 8 full passes on microplane)

### Phase 2 (Cream Stage)

Dairy

- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 pint half-and-half
- 1 pint heavy cream
- ⅓ tsp. nutmeg (grated, 4 full passes on microplane)

## Instructions

### Phase 1: Make the base mix

1. Separate the egg yolks from the egg whites and place the yolks in a 2-quart container
2. Combine the rum, cognac, and bourbon in a separate vessel and set aside
3. Add the milk to the egg yolks along with the sugar and nutmeg and blend with a stick blender until smooth. Try to keep the head of the blender well-submerged so that you whip up as little foam as possible
4. With the motor of the blender still running, slowly pour the spirits into the yolk mixture and blend until fully incorporated
5. Place the lid on the container and refrigerate for 30 days (I typically transfer the mix to a 4-quart jar to stash long term in the fridge)
6. During the first phase aging, remember to shake the jars from time to time

### Phase 2: Add the cream

1. Remove the phase 1 mixture from the fridge and pour into a single vessel that will hold at least 3 quarts
2. Place the stick blender into the mixture and blend on medium speed
3. With the motor running, slowly add the milk, half-and-half, heavy cream, and nutmeg and blend until smooth (the order doesn't matter — you could also combine them all and add at once)
4. Divide the eggnog into a few smaller containers (pint glass jars) that seal tightly and refrigerate for 50–60 days
5. Again, occasional shaking will help keep things nice and homogenized

### Serve

1. Serve straight from the fridge and remember, it's tasty but strong
2. As for long term storage, I've kept unopened jars for 6-12 months without issue

## Notes

I usually just make a triple batch so it uses the entire 750ml liquor bottles. I've never had an issue with giving the drinks away to friends, they always ask for more!
