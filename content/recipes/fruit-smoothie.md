## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 (about 16 ounces)

## Ingredients

Dairy

- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup milk

Produce

- 1 cup frozen fruit (whatever your favorite combo is)
- ½ lime (peeled)

Pantry

- ¼ cup cranberry juice

## Instructions

1. Combine everything in a blender and blitz until smooth

## Notes

The milk keeps it creamy, while the cranberry juice brightens it up without making it taste like a milkshake
