## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 3 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 3 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 drink

## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz. reposado tequila
- ½ oz. mezcal
- 1 barspoon agave nectar
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Flamed orange peel for garnish

## Instructions

1. Add the tequila, mezcal, agave nectar and Angostura bitters to an Old Fashioned glass with one large ice cube
2. Stir until well-chilled
3. Flame an orange peel over the top of the drink to express its oils
4. Garnish with the peel
