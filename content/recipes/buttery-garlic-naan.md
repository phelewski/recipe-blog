## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus 1 hour rising)
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 6-8 naan breads

## Ingredients

**Dough:**

- ½ cup (118ml) warm water (95°F / 35°C), plus more if needed
- 2 Tbsp (25g) granulated sugar
- 2.5 tsp (9g) instant yeast
- ½ cup (112g) full fat yogurt
- 3 Tbsp (45g) vegetable oil
- 1.5 tsps (11g) fine sea salt
- 2.5 cups (380g) bread flour
- Cooking spray
- Bench flour

**Buttery Garlic Topping:**

- ¼ cup (60g) ghee
- 3 cloves (21g) garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon (3g) finely chopped cilantro

## Instructions

1. In a measuring glass, combine the warm water, sugar, and instant yeast. Whisk together until dissolved.
2. Whisk in the yogurt, oil, and salt until evenly combined.
3. Add the flour to a large bowl and pour in the liquid ingredients. Mix by hand until you get a rough dough. If all the flour is not incorporating into the dough, add 1 tablespoon of water at a time until the dough comes together.
4. Transfer the dough to a clean work surface and knead for 3 minutes, or until the dough is smooth. Roll the dough into a ball and place in a medium bowl that is lightly greased with cooking spray.
5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap, and let rise at room temperature for one hour, or in the refrigerator overnight. The dough should be puffy and almost doubled in size.
6. Punch down the dough and divide into 8 even pieces, about 87g per piece of dough. Roll each piece of dough into a ball, cover with a clean kitchen towel and rest for 5 minutes.
7. Lightly flour a clean work surface and roll each piece of dough into a circle that is approximately ⅛-inch thick. If the dough isn't rolling easily, let rest 10 more minutes before trying again. Place the finished dough on a plate and top with a piece of wax paper. Continue rolling the remaining dough, making sure to place a piece of wax paper between each piece of dough.
8. In a small saucepan, melt the ghee over low heat until it reaches a temperature of 200°F / 93°C. Cut off the heat and add the garlic. Once the ghee cools slightly, add the cilantro.
9. To make with a cast iron pan, heat a medium sized cast iron skillet on medium high heat. Once the pan is just about smoking, place a piece of naan dough on the skillet and cook for 30-40 seconds or until puffed and browning on the first side. Flip and cook for another 30 seconds or until browned on the second side. Immediately brush with garlic ghee and repeat with the remaining pieces of dough. Keep warm by holding on a plate in the oven or covering in foil.

## Notes/Tips

- The dough can be made ahead and refrigerated overnight for a slower rise, which develops more flavor.
- Make sure your cast iron skillet is properly preheated — it should be just about smoking before adding the dough. This is key to getting that authentic puffed-up naan texture.
- The garlic ghee can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator. Reheat gently before using.
- You can add other toppings like sesame seeds, nigella seeds (kalonji), or chopped parsley along with the cilantro.
- Store leftover naan in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days. Reheat in a skillet over medium heat before brushing with fresh garlic ghee.
