## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 1 hour 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Total Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes (plus marinating time)
- **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Chicken Souvlaki

- 900g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 125g olive oil
- 15g kosher salt
- 30g garlic (minced, about 5–6 cloves)
- 5g dried oregano
- 5g black pepper (ground)
- 100g Dijon mustard
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 30g lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- Skewers for grilling

### Pita Bread

- 350g all-purpose flour
- 7g instant yeast
- 5g kosher salt
- 8g sugar
- 15g olive oil
- 210g flavorful yeasty beer, warmed to 86–90°F/30–32°C

### Tzatziki Sauce

- 160g English cucumber (grated)
- 200g plain Greek yogurt
- 75g mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic (minced)
- 5g kosher salt
- 5g fresh dill (chopped)
- 15–20g lemon juice (about ½ lemon)

### Tomato-Onion Relish

- 120g tomatoes (diced)
- 60g red onion (finely diced)
- 20g fresh parsley (chopped)

## Instructions

### Make the Tzatziki Sauce

1. Grate the cucumber on a box grater, wrap in paper towels and squeeze out as much excess moisture as you can
2. Combine all tzatziki sauce ingredients and mix to combine
3. This will last in the refrigerator for 3–5 days

### Make the Tomato-Onion Relish

1. Combine the tomatoes, red onion, and parsley and mix thoroughly
2. This will last in the refrigerator for 1–3 days

### Marinate the Chicken

1. If using wood skewers, soak them for a few hours before grilling
2. Cut the chicken thighs into roughly 1-inch pieces
3. To the chicken, add olive oil, salt, garlic, oregano, black pepper, Dijon, lemon zest and juice. Mix well
4. Refrigerate to marinate for at least 30 minutes, up to 24 hours

### Make the Pita Bread

1. While chicken marinates, heat your beer to 98°F/36°C in a small saucepan
2. In a food processor, combine flour, yeast, salt, sugar, and olive oil. Pulse about 5 times to mix
3. With the processor running, slowly stream in the warmed beer until the dough forms (it will be shaggy)
4. Flip the dough onto a floured surface and knead for about 1–2 minutes until it becomes slightly elastic
5. Cut the dough into 4 equal pieces (about 150g each). Shape each into a ball, cover with a damp paper towel, and let them rest at room temperature for 15 minutes
6. After 15 minutes, lightly flour a surface and flatten a dough ball with your hands slightly. Then switch to a rolling pin and roll dough into a 6–8 inch round. Repeat with the remaining 3 dough balls
7. Once the dough is rolled out, allow the pita to rise at room temperature for 30 minutes

### Grill the Pita and Chicken

1. Preheat your grill to 550°F/285°C
2. Assemble the marinated chicken onto the soaked skewers (this should make roughly 6–8 skewers)
3. Using a can of pan spray or a towel soaked in oil, lubricate your grill grates well and grill the chicken and pita alongside each other
4. Grill the pitas on one side until the top begins to bubble up and you can see the edge browning. Flip the pita and cook for about two minutes (about 2 minutes per side)
5. Grill the pitas one at a time so you have plenty of room to grill the chicken
6. Place the skewers on the other side of the grill from the pita. Grill the chicken for 12–15 minutes, flipping every few minutes until the outside is well caramelized but not burnt and the chicken is fully cooked to at least 170°F/76°C
7. Be sure to use separate utensils for the chicken and the pita to avoid cross-contamination

### Assemble the Souvlaki Wrap

1. Remove the chicken from the skewers and cut it into smaller pieces (run your knife through the chicken 2–3 times)
2. Place the chicken in a pita and top with tzatziki sauce, tomato-onion relish, and pepperoncini
