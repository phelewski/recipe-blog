## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 drink

## Ingredients

- 2 oz. bourbon
- ¾ oz. lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
- ½ oz. simple syrup
- ½ oz. egg white (optional)
- Angostura bitters for garnish

## Instructions

1. Add the bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup and egg white (if using) to a shaker
2. Dry-shake for 30 seconds without ice
3. Add ice and shake again for 15–20 seconds until well-chilled
4. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice, or into a coupe without ice
5. Garnish with 3–4 drops of Angostura bitters
