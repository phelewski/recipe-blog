## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus 1 hour chilling)
- **Cook Time:** 14 minutes
- **Total Time:** 34 minutes (plus chilling time)
- **Servings:** Approximately 4 dozen cookies

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 large egg
- 500g all-purpose flour
- 80g dark rye flour
- 1 Tbsp cocoa powder
- 3 tsp ground ginger
- ½ tsp Chinese five-spice powder
- Freshly microplaned nutmeg, to taste
- 1 tsp crushed pink peppercorns
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 2 ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- ¾ cup molasses
- 2 tsp miso paste
- 1 cup powdered (confectioners') sugar
- 3 tsp water

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together all-purpose flour, dark rye flour, cocoa powder, ground ginger, Chinese five-spice powder, freshly microplaned nutmeg, baking soda, and cinnamon until well combined. Set aside.
2. In a separate bowl, cream the softened butter and granulated sugar together until light and fluffy.
3. Mix in the molasses, miso paste, and egg until the mixture is smooth and well blended.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix just until a dough forms — be careful not to overmix.
5. Wrap the dough and chill it in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.
6. Roll out the chilled dough on a lightly floured surface. Place your printed cookie pattern on top and cut out the cookies using a cookie cutter. Arrange the cut cookies on a non-stick sheet tray, leaving a little space between each one.
7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 9-14 minutes. Remove from the oven and allow the cookies to cool completely on a wire rack before glazing.
8. In a small bowl, mix the powdered sugar with water until smooth. Adjust the ratio for your desired consistency — thicker for a heavier coat, thinner for a lighter drizzle. Glaze the cooled cookies and allow the glaze to set before serving or storing.
