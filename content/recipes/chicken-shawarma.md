## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Total Time:** 55 minutes (plus marinating time)
- **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

Meat

- 2¼–2½ lbs chicken thighs (boneless and skinless, or skin on)

Shawarma Marinade

- 2 Tbsp. ground cumin
- 2 Tbsp. ground coriander
- 6–8 garlic cloves (finely minced)
- 2 tsp. salt (use 1 tsp per pound of chicken)
- ¼ tsp. cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp. turmeric
- 1 tsp. ground ginger
- 1 tsp. ground black pepper
- 1 tsp. allspice
- 6 Tbsp. olive oil

## Instructions

### Marinate the chicken

1. Place all marinade ingredients in a bowl and mix, or pulse in a food processor to make a paste
2. Rub the chicken on all sides with the marinade and let it sit for at least 20 minutes (or up to 24–48 hours for the best flavor, refrigerated)
3. You can also cut the chicken into 1-inch cubes and marinate for skewers (soak wood skewers in water for at least 30 minutes first if using)

### Cook the chicken

1. Grill on a preheated grill over medium-high heat, closing the BBQ lid, until all sides have nice grill marks, about 8 minutes per side
2. Move to a cooler part of the grill or finish cooking in a 350°F oven until cooked all the way through (175°F internal), about 10–15 minutes
3. _Alternatively, bake the chicken thighs in a 375°F oven for 30–40 minutes or use a grill pan on the stovetop_

### Serve

1. Taste and adjust salt to taste
2. Enjoy over Israeli salad, tabouli, Lebanese slaw, or with Middle Eastern rice and veggies
3. Or serve as a sandwich with tahini yogurt, tzatziki, pita bread, tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion

## Notes

- I prefer thighs because they are pretty hard to overcook/dry out. Feel free to use boneless skinless chicken breasts, understanding grilling time will shorten
- If using skewers: marinate the cubes of chicken in a bowl for at least 20 minutes before skewering
