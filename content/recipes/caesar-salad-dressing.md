## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 10 minutes
- **Servings:** 10 (makes about 1⅓ cups)

## Ingredients

Dairy

- ½ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

Pantry

- 2 small cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp. anchovy paste (see note)
- 2 Tbsp. freshly squeezed lemon juice (from one lemon)
- 1 tsp. Dijon mustard (Maille brand recommended)
- 1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup mayonnaise (best quality such as Hellmann's or Duke's)
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the garlic, anchovy paste, lemon juice, Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce
2. Add the mayonnaise, Parmigiano-Reggiano, salt and pepper and whisk until well combined
3. Taste and adjust to your liking, then serve or refrigerate until ready to use

## Notes

### Anchovy Paste

Anchovy paste can be found near the canned tuna in the supermarket

### Storage

The dressing will keep well in the refrigerator for about a week and will thicken as it chills. If needed, whisk in a teaspoon or two of water to thin it to your desired consistency

### To Make a Caesar Salad

Toss chopped romaine with enough dressing to generously coat the leaves. Add homemade croutons and freshly grated or shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano, and toss again. Serve immediately
