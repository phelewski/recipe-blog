## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 1 drink

## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz. bourbon
- ½ oz. oloroso sherry (Lustau)
- ¾ oz. lemon juice (fresh)
- ½ oz. grenadine
- 2-3 oz. sparkling water
- Mint sprig for garnish

## Instructions

1. Add the bourbon, sherry, lemon juice, and grenadine to a shaker tin with ice
2. Shake vigorously for 15 seconds
3. Double strain into a low ball glass filled with fresh ice
4. Top off with sparkling water
5. Garnish with a mint sprig
