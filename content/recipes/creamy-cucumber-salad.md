## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes (draining)
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes
- **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

Produce

- 2 large English or seedless cucumbers (sliced thin)
- ½ cup Vidalia or sweet onion (finely sliced)
- 3 Tbsp. fresh dill (minced, divided)

Pantry

- ¼ cup sour cream
- 2 Tbsp. mayonnaise
- 1 Tbsp. white wine vinegar
- ½ tsp. sugar
- 1¼ tsp. kosher salt
- ¼ tsp. black pepper

## Instructions

### Salt and dry the cucumbers

1. Salt the cucumber and onion well with 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and place in a colander over a bowl to drain for 30 minutes
2. Do not rinse; pat dry with lint-free paper towels

### Make the dressing

1. While the cucumbers sit, mix sour cream, mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, 2 tablespoons dill, remaining salt, and pepper in a small bowl

### Assemble

1. Mix the dressing with the dried vegetables
2. Serve chilled with more minced dill on top to garnish
