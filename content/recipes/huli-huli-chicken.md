## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes (plus marinating time)
- **Servings:** 6 (enough for about 3 chickens, split in half)

## Ingredients

Pantry

- ⅓ cup ketchup
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup honey
- ¼ cup sherry
- 1–2 Tbsp. sesame oil
- Worcestershire sauce to taste
- Sriracha or Asian chili paste (or red pepper flakes) to taste

Produce

- 1–2 pieces ginger root (crushed)
- 3 cloves garlic (crushed)
- 1 lemon (squeezed, if available)

## Instructions

### Make the marinade

1. In a bowl, combine all ingredients and mix well. Don't be fussy about measuring — just do them to taste

### Marinate the chicken

1. Brush the chicken with the marinade and let it sit for at least 30 minutes (or longer if you have time)
2. The recipe is enough for about 3 chickens, split in half. You can use chicken pieces if that's what you have

### Grill the chicken

1. Cook the chicken over a grill, brushing with the remaining marinade while cooking
2. Don't forget to huli (turn) the chicken!
