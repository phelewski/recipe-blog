## Timing

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Total Time:** 35 minutes
- **Servings:** 2 wraps

## Ingredients

Produce

- 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped

Pantry

- 2 large flour tortillas
- ¾ cup mayonnaise
- 3 tablespoons pickle brine
- 1 garlic clove (minced)
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Meat

- 2 pieces chicken (oven grilled, sliced)

## Instructions

### Make the Pickle Brine Caesar Dressing

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, pickle brine, garlic, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper until creamy
2. Depending on the acidity of your pickle brine, you might want to add a bit of sweetener to balance it out

### Assemble the Wraps

1. Toss the romaine lettuce into some of the dressing
2. Place each tortilla on a piece of parchment paper and top with dressed salad
3. Add the sliced grilled chicken on top
4. Drizzle with more dressing
5. Wrap tightly, slice in half, and serve
